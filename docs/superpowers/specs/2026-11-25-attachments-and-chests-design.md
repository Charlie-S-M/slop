# Attachments and boss chests

Roadmap item 7, from the person's own list:
- Every weapon, Commons included, can equip a scope, grip, barrel, magazine, and keychain.
- Each part changes the gun's look, and the equip preview shows its effect.
- Parts carry over when a gun evolves.
- Every boss clear, repeats and Tower bosses included, gives a chest: 95% coins, 5% attachment.
- An attachment rolls on a scrolling reel that slows and stops on the part. Rarity odds are
  Common 50, Uncommon 25, Rare 15, Epic 5, Legendary 3.9, Mythic 1, Godly 0.1 (%).
- Attachment Luck in the upgrade menu improves the reel's rarity; the 5% stays fixed.
- Legendary+ results play impact frames.

The person asked for the whole checklist to run without stopping, so the calls below are the
builder's.

## Goals

- **Catalog:** 5 slots × 7 rarities = 35 parts, generated by `Shared/Attachments.luau`. Names are
  a rarity prefix plus the slot: Rusty, Sturdy, Tuned, Arcane, Gilded, Crimson, Prismatic. Each
  part has one effect:

  | Slot | Effect |
  |------|--------|
  | Barrel | Power |
  | Scope | Power, at half the bonus |
  | Grip | Speed |
  | Magazine | Speed, at half the bonus |
  | Keychain | Training |

  The full bonus by rarity is +2, 4, 7, 11, 16, 24, 35%.
- **Ownership:** `Profile.attachments: { [id]: count }` holds parts not on a gun. Equipped parts
  live on the gun: `GunItem.parts: { [slot]: id }`.
  - Equipping takes one from the pile; replacing a part returns the old one.
  - Selling a gun returns its parts.
  - `GunStats.evolve` copies `parts`.
- **One function applies the effect:** `GunStats.effective(item)` returns the gun with its parts
  applied. Both the server (`Combat`) and the client (`ClientState.equipped`) read the equipped gun
  through it, so damage, fire rate, and training all match what the preview shows.
- **Looks:** `GunBuilder.build(def, parts?)` adds a brick per part in its rarity color:
  - a scope on top;
  - a grip under the barrel;
  - a muzzle extension at the tip;
  - a magazine under the body;
  - a charm hanging from the stock.
- **Chests:** every boss kill rolls `Balance.CHEST_PART_CHANCE = 0.05`.
  - A miss gives coins: `enemyCoins(stage) × 8`, with the Tower bonus.
  - A hit rolls a rarity on the reel weights, then a random part of that rarity.
  - The server sends `ChestOpened { kind, coins?, partId?, rarity? }`.
- **Reveal:** a chest pops open on screen.
  - Coins: a coin burst.
  - A part: a horizontal reel of rarity cards scrolls for about 3.5 seconds and eases onto the
    result, then shows a card.
  - Legendary+ results play `ImpactFrames` with a brick model of the part placed in front of the
    camera.
- **Attachment Luck:** an upgrade, "Part Luck". Each level multiplies the weights of Rare and up
  by `1 + luck × (tier − 2)`, then the weights are normalized.
- **UI:** a Backpack "Parts" tab.
  - It shows the equipped gun's five slots, with Unequip.
  - It lists owned parts with their counts.
  - Picking a part shows its effect as a preview, e.g. "Starforge Power 1K → 1.07K", with an
    Equip button for the equipped gun.

## Non-goals

No crafting, trading, or part leveling. Parts equip only on the currently equipped gun; switch
guns to move parts.

## Anti-goals

- The 5% chest chance never changes with luck.
- A part can't be duplicated by equipping or unequipping. Counts are checked on the server.
